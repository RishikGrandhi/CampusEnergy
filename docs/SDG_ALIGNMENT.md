# Sustainable Development Goal alignment

The primary alignment is **SDG 7 — Affordable and Clean Energy**: forecasts and likely high-use-period detection can support better-informed building energy-management decisions.

Supporting alignment includes **SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities**, **SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production**, and **SDG 13 — Climate Action**. The system can support decisions that may reduce unnecessary electricity consumption and associated emissions; this prototype does not measure or claim a quantified reduction.
